## Style

Warm cozy rural illustration with believable texture. Stardew-like warmth without becoming pixel art or chibi. Soft natural light, hand-tended spaces, friendly but not saccharine.

## Palette

Pear gold, leaf green, cream, weathered wood, soft earth brown, late-afternoon amber.

## Character Direction

People look grounded in daily work. Clothes are practical, patched, and personal. Faces should feel kind but specific, not stock-cute.

## Background Direction

Visual novel background, wide and calm. Pathways, orchard rows, cottages, and sheds should guide the eye while keeping the bottom third quiet enough for text overlays.

## Negative

- no glossy mobile-game UI art
- no chibi/anime exaggeration
- no readable signs/text
- no fantasy armor
- no empty generic meadow
