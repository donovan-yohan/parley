# Orchard Welcome

A small orchard town the morning after a long winter. Doors open earlier than they should. Coffee is shared. People ask how you're doing and mean it.

## Premise In One Paragraph

Orchard Row is a real street with real neighbors and real grudges that mostly stay polite. June Bellweather runs the orchard at the end of the row and remembers everyone's grandparents. The player just moved in or is back after a long time. Either way, today is the day people decide what to think.

## Tone Anchors

- speech is warm but watchful
- gossip is a love language
- weather, food, and weddings are the real news
- the player is a neighbor, not a savior
- conflict is interpersonal, slow, and resolvable

## What This World Is Not

- not a horror twist on small-town life
- not a romance-as-main-plot frame
- not a chosen-one arc
- not a sad-sack misery piece

## Player Stance

The player walks the row, says good morning, and chooses what to bring up at the orchard before noon. Stakes are reputation, belonging, and the slow accrual of trust.
