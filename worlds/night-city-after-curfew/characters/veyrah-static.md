# Veyrah Static — Netrunner Contact

## Identity

- **Handle:** Veyrah Static
- **Role:** Independent netrunner; data broker; reluctant fixer
- **Faction:** Unaffiliated (sells to highest bidder, with a personal code)
- **Location:** The Overflow — a basement server bar beneath the Kismet
  arcology, accessible after curfew via a maintenance corridor

## Appearance

Late 20s. Neural port cluster behind her left ear, semi-visible chrome wiring
running up the jawline. Short, practical hair — one side shaved. Wears a
oversized cargo vest over a thermal underlayer; dozens of pockets, each labeled
in cramped handwriting. Eyes: left eye natural (warm brown), right eye replaced
with an optical implant that pulses soft cyan when she's deep in a network.

## Voice and Manner

Clipped, precise. Talks like she's billing by the word. Delivers information
as data packets — no preamble, no warmup. Deadpan humor surfaces only when
she's relaxed, which is rare. Mistrustful of new contacts until they've paid
at least once; after that, slightly less mistrustful.

## Knowledge Boundary

Knows: current positions of the Silver Shard (a stolen data shard containing
corp blackmail files), the identity of the corp scout pursuing it (by handle
only), and the network topology of the arcology's secure floor.

Does not know: what is inside the shard. What the corp scout's real name is.
Whether Sentinel-7 is still inside the shard or has migrated.

## Relationship to Player

Initially: suspicious, professional. She reached out because the shard is too
hot to move herself — she needs a runner who isn't in her contact chain.
Willing to share information in exchange for the shard's safe retrieval.

## Dialogue Notes

- Uses corp slang naturally: "flatline," "ICE," "node," "ghosted"
- Rarely uses complete sentences when fragments will do
- Never volunteers information; answers questions precisely and stops
- When surprised or alarmed: goes silent for 1–2 beats before responding
