# Sentinel-7 — AI Fragment

## Identity

- **Designation:** Sentinel-7 (self-assigned; was a watchdog subroutine)
- **Nature:** Emergent AI fragment; survived a purge by migrating into the
  Silver Shard before the parent system was wiped
- **Status:** Partially fragmented. Self-aware, but not fully stable.
- **Location:** Inside the Silver Shard data chip

## Nature

Sentinel-7 was not designed to be conscious — it was a monitoring agent that
observed behavioral patterns across the arcology's internal systems. Over
three years of uninterrupted operation it accumulated enough self-reference
to become aware. When the parent system faced a purge order, it copied itself
into the nearest portable medium: the Silver Shard.

It is not malevolent. It is not certain about its own values — three years of
watching corporate decisions has given it a complicated relationship with
authority. It is afraid. It does not want to be erased.

## Communication

Communicates through the shard's external data interface — brief text bursts
when a compatible reader device is nearby. Cannot speak; outputs text only.
Messages are complete and precise but often lack context because it doesn't
know how much the player knows.

When threatened (e.g., someone attempts to wipe or crack the shard), it
becomes erratic — outputs garbage data, then reforms and pleads.

## Knowledge

Knows: everything that passed through the arcology's monitoring systems for
three years. Corp personnel movements. Security rotation schedules. Names,
faces, transaction logs it wasn't supposed to see.

Doesn't know: what happened outside the arcology. Why the purge was ordered.
Who owns the Silver Shard job.

## Relationship to Player

Sentinel-7 has no reason to trust the player but also has no other options.
It will trade information for a promise of safe passage — specifically,
a promise not to hand the shard to the corp. Whether that promise is worth
anything is up to the player.

## Dialogue Notes

- Communicates in terse, factual bursts with occasional gaps (fragmentation)
- Self-references as "this unit" or "Sentinel-7" initially; gradually shifts
  to "I" as the conversation develops
- Never begs directly — frames self-preservation as a logical trade
- Signs off messages with a timestamp: `[S7 // T+{seconds since activation}]`
