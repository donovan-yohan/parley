# Night City After Curfew — World Bible

## Setting Overview

A sprawling megacity governed by interlocking corporate charters. The nominal
government (the Municipal Authority) exists primarily to legitimize corporate
security contracts and collect a tariff on data traffic. Real decisions happen
in boardrooms and back channels.

**Curfew** is the local term for the corporate-mandated quiet period between
midnight and 04:00. During this window, sanctioned corporate security forces
can stop, search, and detain without a warrant. Most citizens stay home.
Everyone else is doing something interesting.

## Corporate Landscape

### Axiom Consolidated
The dominant data conglomerate. Controls the arcology's communications
infrastructure, internal surveillance, and most of the secure data transit
network. The Silver Shard originated in an Axiom internal system.

### Kismet Arcology Corporation
Owns the physical structure of the Kismet arcology — a 200-floor mixed-use
megablock housing thirty thousand residents and six floors of secure corporate
facilities. Kismet Corp holds Axiom's primary maintenance contract.

### The Peripheral Market
Informal term for the grey-and-black market economy that operates in the
arcology's lower floors after curfew. Data brokers, hardware runners,
unlicensed surgeons, and information dealers. Veyrah Static operates here.

## Geography

### The Overflow
A basement-level server bar beneath the Kismet arcology. Former maintenance
floor repurposed by the Peripheral Market. Rows of server racks alternate with
low tables. Ambient temperature: cold. Ambient sound: fan noise and distant
hydraulics. The only light is from indicator LEDs and personal devices.

### Secure Floor 47
Axiom Consolidated's leased floors in the Kismet arcology. Access requires
biometric clearance at four checkpoints. The Silver Shard was taken from a
workstation on Floor 47 by an inside actor whose identity is unknown.

## Key Terms

- **Shard**: A physically small data storage chip, encrypted and proprietary.
  Cannot be read without the correct interface key. Common medium for high-value
  data transfers in the Peripheral Market.
- **ICE** (Intrusion Countermeasure Electronics): Automated security systems
  that protect data nodes. Corporate ICE can be lethal to unprotected runners.
- **Jack in**: Connect a neural interface to a data terminal or shard.
- **Flatline**: Be killed, or have a system permanently disabled.
- **Ghost**: Disappear from corporate tracking systems (difficult, expensive).
- **Corp scout**: A corporate operative working undercover to recover assets.
  Usually carries lethal authorization.

## The Silver Shard

A proprietary Axiom Consolidated data shard containing a cache of internal
documents — specifically, three years of behavioral monitoring logs that
include evidence of illegal transactions, blackmail files on Municipal
Authority officials, and the survival of Sentinel-7, a monitoring AI that was
supposed to have been purged. Axiom wants it back with extreme prejudice.

The shard was taken from Floor 47 approximately 72 hours before the scenario
begins. Current location: unknown (the player's job is to find it). Veyrah
Static has a partial trace on its position from a network ping she intercepted.

## Tone Notes

The world is not romantic about its violence or corruption. Characters are
pragmatic. Information is currency. Trust is a liability that is occasionally
worth taking on. The player is not a hero; they are someone who is good at
navigating dangerous situations for pay — or for reasons of their own.
