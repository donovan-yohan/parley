# Background Prompt — Silver Shard Extraction

## Scene

A basement-level server bar in a megacity arcology, photographed from the
interior looking toward the main corridor entrance. The space is cold and dark,
lit almost entirely by the blue-green glow of server rack indicator LEDs and
a single cyan neon strip mounted above the doorway.

## Visual Specifications

- **Environment type:** Underground server facility repurposed as an informal
  meeting space. Functional brutalism. Exposed conduit. Low ceiling.
- **Composition:** Wide establishing shot from inside the room toward the door.
  The corridor visible through the doorway should be dark with a distant
  magenta neon advertisement panel just visible at the end.
- **Time:** Post-midnight. No natural light.
- **Lighting:** Cold LED blue-green from server racks left and right, single
  cyan overhead neon, distant magenta glow from the corridor, deep shadows
  everywhere else.
- **Landmarks:**
  - Server racks with blinking status LEDs along both side walls
  - Low tables made from repurposed equipment cases
  - Cable bundles running along the ceiling and floor
  - A door-frame with peeling security decals and a broken scanner unit
- **Atmosphere:** Tense, quiet, functional. Not dramatic — this is a working
  space where dangerous people do quiet business.
- **Safe overlay zones:** Lower third, center lower third.
- **Negative:**
  - No visible characters or silhouettes
  - No readable text on signs or screens
  - No UI elements or HUD overlays
  - No warm lighting tones (no fire, no incandescent)
  - No exterior daylight
