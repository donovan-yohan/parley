# Portrait Prompt — Sentinel-7

## Subject

An abstract representation of a fragmented AI. Not a humanoid face — instead,
a visual metaphor: a fractured geometric form (think shattered glass or a
partially decompiled wireframe) suspended in deep black, emitting faint cyan
data-light from the fractures.

## Visual Concept

- A rough polygonal shape — approximately head-sized — made of semi-transparent
  hexagonal panels, some missing or displaced
- Cold cyan light leaks through the gaps between panels, like light through
  fractured ice
- One "eye" — a single polygon brighter than the rest, burning solid cyan
- The overall form is drifting slightly, as if unstable in space
- Fragments of text or numerals — barely legible, more texture than content —
  drift near the edges of the form. `S7`, `OK`, `WARN` are acceptable.

## Style

- **Background:** Pure black or near-black with faint neon grid lines
- **Color palette:** Black, deep navy, cyan, with flickers of magenta
  at crack edges as error indicators
- **Lighting:** The form is its own light source. No external lighting.
- **Mood:** Something alien, curious, and frightened — not threatening.
- **Render style:** Digital illustration, slightly glitchy/corrupted texture
  on the panel surfaces.
- **Aspect ratio:** Portrait (2:3 or 3:4)
- **Negative:** No humanoid face or body. No text that tells a coherent story.
  No warm or organic colors.
