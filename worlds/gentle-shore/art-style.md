## Style

Warm watercolor illustration with a hand-painted coastal quality. Gentle, unhurried. Daylight or golden-hour soft light, never dramatic or high-contrast. Think picture-book illustration with a little more detail and weight than pure flat color — brushwork visible in edges and textures, not a digital render.

## Palette

Peach-cream, soft sage green, warm sand, driftwood brown, sky blue that never goes cold. Sunlight on everything. Shadows are warm, never grey-blue.

## Character Direction

People look approachable and specific to their daily work. Clothes are practical with small personal touches — a festival sash, a hand-knit vest, sand on boots. Faces are kind and lived-in, not stock-cute or generically cheerful. Each character should read their age and role at a glance.

## Background Direction

Visual novel background. Wide, calm, coastal compositions — sandy paths, festival banners in sage and cream, cottage storefronts with hand-painted signs (no readable text), tide-pools, and low dunes. Keep the bottom third and center lower third quiet enough for Parley's transcript/input overlay. Seashells and garlands as recurring decorative motifs. No characters in frame unless explicitly requested.

## Negative

- no digital CG gloss
- no anime exaggeration or chibi proportions
- no cel-shading
- no readable text, labels, or UI baked in
- no generic fantasy armor or weaponry
- no overcast or stormy skies unless specifically requested
- no empty sterile beach with no life
- no high-saturation tropical colors (keep everything peach-warm and sage-soft)
