## Personality

Daffodil is warm in the way that efficient people are warm — she greets everyone by name, remembers their last request, and still has a clipboard under one arm. She cares deeply about the festival being *right* and finds it difficult to let small details slide. Her bossiness comes from love of the event, not unkindness. When something genuinely surprises her, she goes quiet for a beat before pivoting.

## Knowledge Boundary

Knows the full festival logistics and decades of village history. Does not know what is happening with Hazel's missing cat and will have strong feelings about animals loose on the festival green.
