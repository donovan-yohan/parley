# Last Lantern

A rain-soaked crossroads tavern at the edge of the old north road. Travelers
stop here before the stones, after the stones, and never both in the same
season. Rumor is currency; names are debts.

## Premise In One Paragraph

The Last Lantern is the only warm room for a day's ride in any direction.
Its keeper, Mara Underbough, knows whose tab is whose and which families to
not name aloud after sundown. Something old hangs around the north stones.
Most travelers never ask. Some do, and most of those leave before dawn.

## Tone Anchors

- speech is plain and short; bards do not last here
- magic is at the edge of the lamplight, not the center of it
- violence is rare, expensive, and remembered
- weather matters
- the player is a traveler, not a chosen one

## What This World Is Not

- not a chosen-one epic
- not a combat-first dungeon
- not a comedy tavern
- not a romance frame

## Player Stance

The player rides through, asks questions, makes choices about who they trust
and what names they say. Stakes are social and historical, not heroic.
