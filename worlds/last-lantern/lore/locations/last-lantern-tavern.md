# Last Lantern Tavern

The Last Lantern sits near the old north road. It is warm, crowded, and tense
when names tied to old debts are spoken too loudly.

## Established In The MVP

- The player can ask who remembers the old north road.
- Mara Underbough answers as a local tavernkeep with bounded rumor knowledge.
- The Ashford name and the north stones remain unresolved story threads.
