## Style

Grounded fantasy. Painted with weight. Lantern-lit, rain-soft. Reference: late-1800s tavern interiors crossed with early-modern oil portrait. Brushwork visible. Hands and faces specific.

## Palette

Warm umber, rust, lamp-yellow inside; cold blue night through windows. Damp wool, wet leather, smoke-darkened wood, tarnished copper.

## Character Direction

People look like people. Wear shows. Posture is tired more often than heroic. Eye contact is plausible and uneven, not the dead-center stare of stock art. Wardrobe communicates work, class, weather, and region.

## Background Direction

Visual novel background. Wide, readable, atmospheric tavern and crossroads compositions. Keep the bottom third and center lower third relatively quiet so Parley's transcript/input overlay can sit on top. No characters in frame unless explicitly requested. No readable text, labels, or UI baked into the image.

## Negative

- no glossy CG render
- no anime / chibi / cel-shading
- no cape-and-armor heroics
- no modern eyewear, zippers, polyester
- no identifiable real-world celebrities
- no high-fantasy elf/dwarf shorthand unless the character is one
- no perfect symmetry
- no readable signage or UI text
