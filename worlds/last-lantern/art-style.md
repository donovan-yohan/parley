## Style

Grounded fantasy. Painted with weight. Lantern-lit, rain-soft.
Reference: late-1800s tavern interiors crossed with early-modern oil portrait.
Brushwork visible. Hands and faces specific.
Light: warm umber, rust, lamp-yellow inside; cold blue night through windows.
Texture: damp wool, wet leather, smoke-darkened wood, tarnished copper.

## Subject Defaults

People look like people. Wear shows. Posture is tired more often than heroic.
Eye contact is plausible and uneven, not the dead-center stare of stock art.

## Negative

- no glossy CG render
- no anime / chibi / cel-shading
- no cape-and-armor heroics
- no modern eyewear, zippers, polyester
- no identifiable real-world celebrities
- no high-fantasy elf/dwarf shorthand unless the character is one
- no perfect symmetry
