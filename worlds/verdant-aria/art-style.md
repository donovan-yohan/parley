## Style

Ornate hand-painted fantasy. Richly detailed. Inspiration: early SNES-era JRPG artwork — painterly menu screens, jewel-toned color fields, gilded borders, pixel-sharp iconography softened into illustration. Weight and texture like illuminated manuscripts, not digital airbrush.

## Palette

Deep navy night sky (#0a0e3d). Royal purple shadows (#5b3387). Ivory and parchment in the light (#f5e8c8). Gold accents for anything important — borders, crests, candle-lit edges (#d4af37). No washed-out midtones; every value earns its place.

## Character Direction

Characters carry history. Clothes are worn with purpose: a bard's doublet with one sleeve rolled for lute-reach, a tavernkeep's apron stained in the right places. Faces are expressive but not exaggerated. Gold jewelry or accessories indicate rank or aspiration. Eyes hold knowledge or weariness, not neutrality.

## Background Direction

Visual novel background. Tavern interiors by candlelight: heavy wooden beams, pewter mugs, wall-mounted iron sconces. Parchment maps on tabletops. Heraldic banners — faded, moth-eaten, still proud. Keep the bottom third and center lower third low-clutter so Parley's transcript overlay reads clearly.

## Typography Reference (for any baked-in titles/labels — avoid in assets)

If text must appear in concept art or style boards: Cinzel (display), Spectral (body). Gold on navy, or navy on ivory.

## Negative

- no cel-shading or modern anime line art
- no photorealistic CG render
- no contemporary clothing or technology
- no identifiable real-world people
- no readable signage or UI text baked into background art
- no high-saturation neon — this palette is jewel-toned, not garish
- no perfect bilateral symmetry on architecture or portraits
- no visible player-character in backgrounds
