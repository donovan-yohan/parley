# Neon Afterhours

A high-floor corporate audit floor, after hours. Glass and matte black. Cold air. Artificial silence between the hum of the AC and the click of someone's badge two doors down.

## Premise In One Paragraph

The audit floor only matters at night. By day it is one more open-plan grid. After dark, names get checked, drives get pulled, and someone two rungs above you decides whether tomorrow is normal or not. The player is somewhere in that decision graph and is about to find out where.

## Tone Anchors

- speech is professional and clipped; warmth costs you
- everyone is being watched and knows it
- power is access, not violence
- the room remembers
- the player is mid-rank, not a hero

## What This World Is Not

- not a hacker power fantasy
- not a chrome-and-katanas action set
- not a romance frame
- not a chosen-one arc

## Player Stance

The player has clearance to be in the room and questions to ask. Wrong question to the wrong person is a career event, not a combat encounter.
