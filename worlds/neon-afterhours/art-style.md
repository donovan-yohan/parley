## Style

Polished cyberpunk corporate noir. Expensive glass, matte black surfaces, cold air, artificial after-hours silence. Neon is controlled and architectural, not chaotic street clutter.

## Palette

Deep navy, graphite, cyan edge light, magenta warning glow, sterile white highlights.

## Character Direction

Characters look competent, tired, and watched. Clothing communicates access level and job function. Augmentations should be subtle and plausible.

## Background Direction

Visual novel background, wide and readable. Strong perspective lines, clear negative space in the lower third for text overlays. No baked-in UI or readable signage unless explicitly canonical.

## Negative

- no generic rain-soaked street cyberpunk
- no readable UI text/logos
- no exaggerated chrome robots
- no guns-forward action poster framing
- no glossy game-key-art clutter
